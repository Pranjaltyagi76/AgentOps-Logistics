## s1. Purpose and scope

This procedure covers shipments that have not yet breached their SLA but are at risk of doing so, as
defined in the Delivery SLA and Escalation Policy.

## s2. Express shipments at risk

When an express shipment is at risk of missing its promised date (in transit within twelve hours of the
end of its promised date with no out-for-delivery scan), escalate proactively with severity medium so
the carrier can prioritise it. Tell the customer the shipment is being prioritised; do not promise it
will arrive on time.

## s3. Standard shipments at risk

Standard shipments at risk do not need escalation until the SLA is breached. Share the latest scan and
any current ETA, and tell the customer you will keep them updated.
