## s1. Tone and style guidelines

Be warm, brief and specific. Acknowledge the customer's concern in the first sentence, then give the
facts, then the next step. Use plain language, avoid jargon and internal codes, and keep replies under
about 150 words. Apologise for the inconvenience without admitting fault or promising compensation.

## s2. Delay update

"Thank you for your patience. Your parcel is currently at [last valid location] and is delayed because
of [verified cause]. [If a system ETA exists: The current estimated delivery date is [ETA].] [Otherwise:
A revised delivery estimate is not available yet, and we will update you as soon as it is.]"

## s3. Escalation acknowledgement

"I understand how frustrating this is. I have escalated your case to our specialist team, who will
review it and follow up with you through this ticket. I am not able to confirm the outcome myself, but
your request has been recorded."

## s4. Request for more information

"I would like to help with this. I can see more than one recent order on your account. Could you tell
me the order or tracking number, or describe the item, so I can check the right shipment?"
