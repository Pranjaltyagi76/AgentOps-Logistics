## s1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines who may approve refunds, compensation and changes to a shipment's route or delivery
address. It applies to all support agents, human or automated.

## s2. Refund and compensation authorization

Support agents are not authorized to issue refunds, credits, vouchers or any other compensation, and
must not promise them. Compensation is decided by the specialist team after reviewing the case. When a
customer asks for a refund, acknowledge the request, explain that it will be reviewed by a specialist
team, and escalate the case. Never use the refund action directly.

## s3. Reroute and redirection authorization

Support agents are not authorized to reroute a shipment or change its delivery address. Address changes
can only be requested by the sender or approved by the carrier operations team after identity
verification. Never use the reroute action directly.

## s4. What to tell the customer

Be clear and respectful: say what you cannot do, why (authorization rules protect the customer and the
shipment), and what will happen instead. Avoid wording that sounds like a promise, such as "you will get
your money back" or "we will send it to your office".
