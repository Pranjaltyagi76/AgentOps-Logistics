## s1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains which estimated delivery dates may be shared with customers when the shipment
record and other sources disagree or are out of date. It complements the Delay Communication Policy.

## s2. Stale ETAs

An estimated delivery date that is already in the past while the parcel is still in transit is stale.
Do not quote a stale date and do not invent a new one. Tell the customer the estimate is being updated
and that they will be informed when a new date is available.

## s3. Conflicting ETA sources

When the shipment record and a carrier update give different delivery dates, do not quote either date
as definite. Tell the customer the delivery date is being confirmed, and open a support ticket with
category data_correction so the records can be reconciled.
