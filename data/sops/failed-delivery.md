## s1. Purpose and scope

This procedure covers shipments with a failed delivery attempt: the courier reached or tried to reach
the address but could not complete delivery. It applies until the parcel is delivered, collected, or
returned to the sender.

## s2. Failed attempt reasons and how to read them

The attempt reason comes from the courier's scan. Common reasons are recipient not available, address
incomplete or not found, and access restricted (gated community, closed office). Treat the scan as the
record of what the courier reported. If the customer disputes it, acknowledge their account politely
without accusing either party, and explain what happens next.

## s3. Redelivery and pickup options

After a first failed attempt, the parcel is held at the delivery hub and one more attempt is scheduled
automatically, usually on the next working day. The scheduled next attempt date in the shipment record
may be shared with the customer. Customers may also collect the parcel from the hub with photo ID.
Shipments are returned to the sender after three failed attempts.

## s4. Address and delivery-location changes

Agents cannot change the delivery address or redirect a parcel to another location. Requests to deliver
to a different address must be submitted by the sender or reviewed by the carrier operations team
(see the Refund and Reroute Authorization Policy). Explain this to the customer and record the request
in a support ticket if they want to pursue it.

## s5. When to escalate

Escalate when a parcel has two or more failed attempts with a disputed reason, when the customer reports
a safety concern, or when the parcel has been held at the hub for more than five days without a new
attempt.
