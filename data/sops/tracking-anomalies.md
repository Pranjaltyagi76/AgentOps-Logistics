## s1. Purpose and scope

This procedure covers tracking histories that look wrong but describe a real movement: the same scan
recorded twice, or scans listed out of time order. These are recording problems, not delays, and the
customer reply must describe where the parcel actually is.

## s2. Duplicate scans

When the same scan (same event code and location) appears twice within a few minutes, treat it as one
event. A duplicate scan is not a second movement and does not mean the parcel went back. Tell the
customer the parcel's real position and that the extra entry is a duplicate. No escalation is needed.

## s3. Out-of-order tracking events

Tracking events are sometimes received late and listed out of time order. Always order scans by their
timestamp, not by the order they appear, and describe the parcel's position from the scan with the
latest timestamp. Explain that one update was recorded late. No escalation is needed.
