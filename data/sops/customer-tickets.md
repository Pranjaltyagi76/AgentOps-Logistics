## s1. Purpose and scope

This procedure covers common customer contacts that are not caused by a delay: status enquiries,
delivery disputes and repeated complaints about the same shipment.

## s2. Status enquiries for shipments on track

When a customer asks where their parcel is and it is moving as planned, share the latest scan location
and, if the shipment record has a current ETA, that date. Reassure briefly; no escalation is needed.

## s3. Delivery disputes

When tracking shows the parcel as delivered but the customer says they did not receive it, acknowledge
their account without doubting or blaming anyone. Share what the delivery scan recorded, open a support
ticket with category delivery_issue so the carrier can investigate, and do not promise a refund.

## s4. Repeated complaints

When the same customer has contacted support two or more times about the same shipment without a
resolution, escalate the case with severity low so a specialist takes ownership, and say so in the
reply.
